## Findings

1. **P1 — Non-obvious feature and purchase duties have been narrowed to discoverability.** `atom-f9a98fc9b0544658a3b9a4b0152a160e` and `atom-89ba978493b44f34a8ed0f9e9d4b9bb1` use “hard-to-discover” as their deciding condition. A visible feature or purchase may still require explanation because its behavior, trigger, fulfillment, or review method is unclear. The private reviewed criteria expressly include those cases. Broaden both criteria and applicability to cover what a reviewer cannot reasonably find **or understand/exercise** from the app alone; preserve the conditional need for supporting documentation. This affects the later metadata/attestation questions as well.

2. **P1 — Third-party compliance is framed mainly as pre-integration assessment.** `atom-f65a3e371c2c49229e0449753fef6859` says to assess external components before integration and retain responsibility for their behavior. The Introduction also places responsibility on the developer for guideline compliance of integrated ads, analytics, SDKs, and services while the app is distributed. State ongoing compliance and reassessment alongside selection. An inventory check alone can only partially decide this obligation.

3. **P2 — Bug-fix path lacks its eligibility boundary and exception link.** `atom-fb4cb3107c0940c49cb5b9268e624813` says only “eligible bug-fix path,” while `req-fb3bf8d317c44b1383032c7a55cf047d` is an unlinked exception. The live rule concerns fixes to apps already distributed through the stated channels, with legal and safety issues excluded from the expedited treatment. Put that boundary in applicability and link the exception to the conditional process atom; otherwise a later attestation question could target an ineligible new app or imply the exception covers legal/safety violations.

4. **P2 — Hardware/resource access needs an accepted demonstration alternative.** `atom-e7ac52bbed6742cea1216b232bf01b1a` can be read as always requiring physical delivery of special hardware. Apple's [App Review preparation guidance](https://developer.apple.com/distribute/app-review/) also accepts a demonstration video in hard-to-replicate cases, while leaving open a later hardware request. Qualify the criterion or applicability so route checks do not fail an otherwise accepted review arrangement. A video does not replace access to ordinary software features.

5. **P3 — Related links omit source-to-duty relationships.** Add `atom-c44cce19b9104b2ca5f658e0dd2a93a5` to `req-dae83badac024810a203dc4386ac989d.related`, `atom-f7d015611dde4efda35fcf052340c2f6` to `req-e8e042e43f2e4af2b0ced7775630f853.related`, and `atom-fb4cb3107c0940c49cb5b9268e624813` to `req-fb3bf8d317c44b1383032c7a55cf047d.related`. These are traceability improvements, not extra obligations.
