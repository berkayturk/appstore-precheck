# Independent guideline source review

Six reviewers compared separate section files with the live Apple guideline page and the private source inventory on 2026-09-25–26. Each reviewer worked outside the section author's worktree. Their quote-free reports are in `docs/independent-review/` after integration. This log records the decisions applied to the public catalog.

| Section | Independent findings | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Safety | Six: overly narrow or broad content criteria; detached mature-content conditions; Kids exceptions appeared uncheckable. | Refined the content tests, linked the conditional web UGC allowance, and removed the misleading non-app routes on Kids conditions. The linked Kids obligations receive evidence-seeking attestation. |
| 2 Performance | Seven: demo access boundaries, unsupported defect log requirement, platform imagery scope, iPad feasibility, HealthKit, downloaded educational code, and Mac update traceability. | Corrected the criteria and source links. A defect log is suggested evidence, not a required artifact. |
| 3 Business | Eight: binding payment conditions hidden as exceptions, a wrong Boolean condition, unrelated check routes, and missing scope or exception links. | Promoted 12 conditions into obligations, retained their allowance records separately, corrected the conjunction, removed eight unrelated route edges, and refined storefront, hardware, loan, and trading scope. |
| 4 Design | Five: subtype applicability, login exception propagation, and two detached allowances. | Narrowed AR, template, remote desktop, keyboard, Safari, and first-launch conditions; propagated login exceptions; linked the payment and approved-feed allowances. |
| 5 Legal | Eight: privacy-source logic, consent basis, health-data benefit conditions, configuration-profile scope, social login, scope-only check routes, VPN/MDM data, and quality signal. | Corrected criteria and links, promoted two benefit conditions to obligations, extended 5.5 applicability to configuration profiles, removed nine unsupported decision edges, and made the quality signal informational. |
| Intro and submission process | Five: discoverability, ongoing third-party responsibility, bug-fix boundary, hardware demonstration, and source links. | Broadened review-note triggers, recorded continuing component responsibility and eligible update conditions, accepted a suitable demonstration alternative, and added trace links. |

The section reviews check source classification and meaning. They do not certify that an app passes any guideline. Existing heuristic routes remain partial; the generic attestation route records a developer answer separately from verified automatic evidence. A later source drift requires another comparison.
